What it checks

Three checks. One risk score.

You get a score from 0 to 100 and the warning signs behind it — so you can see why a profile looks risky.

Face

Whether a face is visible, how clearly it was captured, and how much of the frame it fills.

Image

Whether the picture is large enough to judge, or a small re-shared copy.

Text

The profile's own words, for the patterns behind romance and crypto scams: being pushed to Telegram or WhatsApp, talk of investing or payment apps, paid-content links.

Free and Pro

You get 2 free checks every week. GhostCheck Pro removes the limit — unlimited checks, weekly, monthly or yearly. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings.

What it doesn't do

GhostCheck looks at how a photo was taken — not who is in it. A stolen photo of a real person can still pass every check. It is not a lie detector and cannot prove who anyone is. If something still feels off, ask for a live video call.
