HOW TO USE GHOSTCHECK

From inside the app — open GhostCheck, tap Check a photo, and pick the photo you want to check.

From any other app

  1. Take a screenshot of the profile you want to check.
  2. Open the screenshot and tap the share button.
  3. Choose GhostCheck from the share sheet.

If GhostCheck does not appear in the share sheet, scroll right and tap More, then enable GhostCheck in the list.

A PHOTO WON'T OPEN

If a photo is stored in iCloud and has not been downloaded to your iPhone yet, iOS shows a “Downloading…” message before GhostCheck starts, and it can stall there. That happens before any app can see the photo, so GhostCheck cannot work around it. Open the photo in the Photos app first and wait until it displays fully, then try again. To avoid it altogether, go to Settings → Photos and choose “Download and Keep Originals”.

SUBSCRIPTIONS

To manage or cancel a subscription, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

PRIVACY

GhostCheck analyses photos entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.
