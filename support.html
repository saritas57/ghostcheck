HOW TO USE GHOSTCHECK

  1. Take a screenshot of the profile you want to check.
  2. Open the screenshot and tap the share button.
  3. Choose GhostCheck from the share sheet.

If GhostCheck does not appear in the share sheet, scroll right and tap More, then enable GhostCheck in the list.

SUBSCRIPTIONS

To manage or cancel a subscription, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

PRIVACY

GhostCheck analyses screenshots entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.
