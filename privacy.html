GhostCheck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

GhostCheck does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

WHAT THE APP DOES

When you check a photo with GhostCheck — either by choosing it inside the app or by sharing a screenshot to GhostCheck from another app — the image is analysed on your device using Apple's Vision framework. The analysis happens entirely offline. The image is never uploaded, never sent to a server, and never leaves your device. GhostCheck does not keep a copy of it.

WE DO NOT COLLECT

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No personal information is requested or stored.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers are used.
  • No images or analysis results are transmitted anywhere.

SUBSCRIPTIONS

GhostCheck Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy governs that transaction. The app stores a small record on your device — whether a subscription is currently active and when it expires — so the app and its share extension can share that state. This record never leaves your device.

CHILDREN

GhostCheck does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.
